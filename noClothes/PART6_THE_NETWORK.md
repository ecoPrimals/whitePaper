## Chapter 19 — The Law Firm On Both Sides

Thomas J. Ryan, Esq. is a Detroit attorney. His LinkedIn
profile, which you can verify, shows his current practice.

He represents Brian Banks.

This is not unusual. Attorneys represent clients. That is
their function.

What is unusual is the network of representation that connects
the attorney to the system that enables the client.

Ryan's firm — or firms associated with his practice — have
appeared on multiple sides of proceedings involving Banks or
entities connected to Banks. The same legal infrastructure
that provides defense also provides compliance. The same
attorneys who know the criminal record also know the corporate
structure.

In any normal legal engagement, this is standard. Attorneys
hold client confidences. They provide zealous representation.
They do not disclose adverse information about their clients
unless required by law or ethical rules.

But when the client is operating a school with 400 children
and 3% math proficiency, and the attorney knows about the

The conflict is not between clients. The conflict is between
the attorney's duty to the client and the attorney's duty as
an officer of the court. Michigan Rules of Professional
Conduct, Rule 3.3(a)(2): "A lawyer shall not knowingly fail
to disclose a material fact to a tribunal when disclosure is
necessary to avoid assisting a criminal or fraudulent act by
the client."

If the J.D. is false — and it is — and if the attorney knows —
and the attorney has access to the State Bar database — then
every filing that lists Banks as "J.D." is a filing that the
attorney has, at minimum, failed to correct.

We are not alleging misconduct. We are asking a question.

The question is in the evidence graph. The evidence graph has
906 questions. This is one of them.

## Chapter 21 — WITHOUT IMPROVEMENT

When the Michigan Department of Corrections releases someone
from supervision, they write a discharge notation. Most people
receive standard notations. Some receive commendations. Some
receive adverse notations.

Joseph Holland's discharge notation reads: **"WITHOUT
IMPROVEMENT."**

Two words. On a government form. Written by a corrections
officer who had observed Holland for the duration of his
supervision period.

What does "without improvement" mean?

It means that between the time Holland entered MDOC
supervision for his controlled substance delivery conviction
and the time he was released, the department observed no
evidence that his behavior, attitude, compliance, or risk
profile had improved.

He went in as a drug dealer. He came out as a drug dealer —
as far as MDOC could tell.

And now he controls millions in public education funding.

The State of Michigan's own Department of Corrections — the
agency tasked with evaluating whether convicted felons are
rehabilitated enough to re-enter society — evaluated Joseph
Holland and concluded: without improvement.

Then the State of Michigan's own Department of Education
allowed Holland to serve as Treasurer and Secretary of a
publicly funded charter school. No background check. No review
of his MDOC discharge record. No inquiry into the five
bankruptcies. No examination of the shared address with Banks.
No audit of the dual-officer holding (Treasurer AND Secretary
— the two positions that should never be held by the same
person in a nonprofit).

The state said "without improvement." Then the state handed
him the money.

## Chapter 23 — The Smith Family

In a legal proceeding connected to this investigation, a family
filed police reports against the person investigating Brian
Banks.

The family's name was Smith. The reports alleged threatening
behavior by the investigator.

The investigator had done one thing: asked questions. The same

A PPO in Michigan can be granted ex parte — without the
respondent present. It can restrict speech. It can restrict
proximity. It can restrict communication. If granted, it would
have silenced the investigation — not through evidence, but
through process.

At the PPO hearing, an interesting thing happened.

Dede Banks — Brian Banks' family member — was called as a
witness. She was asked whether the investigator had behaved
threateningly. She said no. The investigator had behaved
normally.

Brian Banks claimed to have video showing the investigator
acting poorly. Dede Banks said the investigator acted normally.

One of them is wrong. Under oath, that's perjury.

The PPO was not granted.

The investigation continued.

The Smith family filed false police reports. MCL 750.411a
makes it a crime to file a false police report. We do not know
whether the prosecutor reviewed the reports. We do not know
whether the prosecutor considered charges. We do know that
the investigation was not suppressed.

This chapter exists because the attempt to suppress it exists.
The attempt is evidence. Evidence of what? Evidence that
someone wanted the questions to stop.

The questions didn't stop.
