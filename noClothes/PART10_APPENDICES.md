## Appendix E — How to Read a Force Graph

The evidence library at detroit.primals.eco renders the data
as an interactive force-directed graph. Here is how to read
it:

**Nodes** are circles. Each circle is a person, entity, or
concept. The size of the circle corresponds to the number of
connections — more connections, bigger circle.

**Colors** indicate node type:
- Investigation subjects (red/orange)
- Institutions (blue)
- Articles (green)
- Historical parallels (gray)
- Commons knowledge (light blue)

**Edges** are lines connecting nodes. Click any edge to see
the relationship type and source.

**Hovering** over a node shows its label and connection count.

**Clicking** a node highlights all its connections and dims
everything else — showing you the immediate neighborhood of
that node.

**Searching** (if available) lets you find any node by name.

**Dragging** nodes lets you rearrange the graph to explore
specific clusters.

The graph is running entirely in your browser. No data is
sent to any server. No analytics. No tracking. No cookies.
Your exploration is private.
