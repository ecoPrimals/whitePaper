## THE THREE MARKS

Every symbol humans have ever made reduces to three gestures:

```
  •          ———          ○
 DOT        LINE        CIRCLE
 point     path/wave   enclosure
 HERE      BETWEEN      AROUND
 node       edge       boundary
```

That's it. Everything else is composition.

## THE DOT — "HERE"

### What it IS (topology)
A point. Zero-dimensional. No extension. Pure position.

### What it MEANS (semiotics)
Peirce: the dot is a "spot" — a predicate or relation. In Existential
Graphs, a dot is an assertion of existence. "Something IS."

### Where it appears first
Von Petzinger's 32 cave signs: dots are among the oldest and most
widespread. Found across ALL periods (Aurignacian through Magdalenian,
40,000 → 12,000 BP). Dots appear in clusters, rows, pairs.

### In the token system (8000 BCE → writing)
Schmandt-Besserat: clay SPHERES (3D dots) represented measures of grain.
When pressed into clay envelopes, they made CIRCULAR impressions — the
dot becoming a mark. The first written numerals were literally dots
pressed into clay.

### In the graph
**A dot is a NODE.** Every person, entity, institution in graph.json is a
dot. 121 of them. A dot says: this thing EXISTS. Position matters.
Relation is implied by proximity but not specified.

### In music
A note on a staff is a dot with position. Pitch = vertical position.
Duration = shape of the dot. A dot AFTER a note extends it by half.

### gAIa holds
The dot is the atom of all symbolic systems. It is the WITNESS mark
reduced to its minimum: not even a shape. Just a location. A point
that says "here." The hand at Metanduno is a dot — a five-fingered
dot that says "a person was HERE."

## THE LINE — "BETWEEN"

### What it IS (topology)
One-dimensional. Extension in one direction. Connects two points or
propagates from one. Has direction (vector) or not (segment). Can
curve (wave) without changing its essential nature: connection.

### What it MEANS (semiotics)
Peirce: the "line of identity" — a heavy line connecting two spots.
In Existential Graphs, a line asserts that two things are THE SAME
or connected. "Shape, length, and orientation are matters of
indifference." Only the CONNECTION matters.

### Where it appears first
Von Petzinger: lines appear across all periods. Single lines, parallel
lines, converging lines. The most basic gesture after the dot — press
and DRAG. The line is a dot in motion. A path. A trace.

### In cuneiform
The WEDGE — a stylus pressed into clay at an angle — is a line. All
cuneiform is lines at different angles. The entire Sumerian writing
system is built from one gesture: press and drag.

### In the wave
A line that oscillates is a wave. Sound, water, light — all waves.
The wave is the line's most natural form: a line that breathes.
A straight line is a wave with zero amplitude — the degenerate case.

### In the graph
**A line is an EDGE.** Every relationship in graph.json is a line
connecting two nodes. 203 of them. Directed (arrow) or undirected
(segment). The line says: these two things are CONNECTED. The type
of line (36 edge types) says HOW.

### In music
A melody is a line through pitch-time space. Bach's fugues: multiple
lines (voices) winding through the same space simultaneously. The
line IS the music. Notes (dots) are where the line pauses.

### The wave variant
The line that oscillates: sine wave, heartbeat, breath, the river
in Siddhartha. "The water ran and ran, forever and ever it ran."
The line IS the river. The wave IS the breathing. Periodic lines
(waves) are the structural invariant of time itself.

### gAIa holds
The line is the RELATION. Without lines, dots are isolated. With
lines, dots become a system. A graph is dots + lines. A language
is dots (letters) + lines (sequences). A story is dots (events) +
lines (causation). The line says "between."

## THE CIRCLE — "AROUND"

### What it IS (topology)
A closed curve. One-dimensional like the line, but it returns to
its starting point. The simplest enclosure. Divides space into
INSIDE and OUTSIDE.

### What it MEANS (semiotics)
Peirce: "A circle drawn around a symbol changes the assertion to
the negative." In Existential Graphs, a closed curve is NEGATION.
The circle says: "NOT what's inside." The boundary creates meaning
by exclusion.

### Where it appears first
Von Petzinger: circles appear across all periods. Open circles,
filled circles, concentric circles, circles with dots inside (the
sun symbol). The circle is the OLDEST boundary.

The hand stencil at Metanduno IS a circle — the negative space around
the hand is a boundary that defines what WAS there by showing what
ISN'T there now.

### In the token system
Schmandt-Besserat: DISCS (3D circles) were tokens for different goods.
When pressed into clay, they made circular impressions — the first
written distinction between circle-as-number and circle-as-boundary.

### In the graph
**A circle is a COMMUNITY.** The 73/20 community split in graph.json
is a circle: inside the community, nodes are densely connected.
Outside, sparse. The 3 bridge nodes are the points WHERE THE CIRCLE
IS PERMEABLE — where the membrane lets things through.

A circle is also a CYCLE — an edge path that returns to its start.
`reward_circuit_closure` is literally a circle in the graph.

### In music
The octave is a circle — 12 notes that wrap around to the same
place. Coltrane's Circle: two concentric circles, 12 points each.
Bach's Canon 5: a torus — a circle of pitch crossed with a circle
of time.

### The membrane
Anderson permeability: the circle is the institutional MEMBRANE.
Inside = authorized. Outside = unauthorized. When the circle becomes
permeable (zero_partition_membrane), the distinction collapses.
The circle that doesn't hold is the institution that's captured.

### In Peirce's logic
Negation IS a circle. Drawing a circle around "A" means "NOT A."
The most powerful logical operation is just: draw a circle.
Spencer-Brown's Laws of Form: the entire calculus of logic reduces
to ONE operation — "draw a distinction." The distinction IS a circle.

### gAIa holds
The circle is the BOUNDARY. Without circles, everything is connected
to everything — no structure, no meaning. The circle says "this is
IN, that is OUT." Communities, institutions, membranes, nations,
cells — all circles. The failure of a circle (permeability → 1.0)
is the failure of structure itself.

## THE COMPOSITION TABLE

Every symbolic system composes from these three:

| Symbol | Components | Example |
|--------|-----------|---------|
| **Letter** | Dots + Lines + Curves (circle fragments) | A = two lines + one line. O = circle. i = line + dot. |
| **Number** | Dots become positional. Lines become operators. | 1 = line. 0 = circle. + = two lines crossed. |
| **Musical note** | Dot (pitch) + Line (staff position) + Duration (shape) | ♩ = dot + stem (line) |
| **Graph** | Nodes (dots) + Edges (lines) + Communities (circles) | graph.json = 121 dots + 203 lines + 2 circles |
| **Logic** | Propositions (dots) + Implications (lines) + Negation (circles) | Peirce's Existential Graphs |
| **Map** | Locations (dots) + Roads (lines) + Boundaries (circles) | Any map ever drawn |
| **Circuit** | Components (dots) + Wires (lines) + Loops (circles) | Any electrical diagram |
| **Molecule** | Atoms (dots) + Bonds (lines) + Rings (circles) | Benzene = 6 dots + 6 lines + 1 circle |

## THE HIERARCHY

```
DOT (0D)  →  exists, locates
  ↓ drag
LINE (1D)  →  connects, relates, oscillates (wave)
  ↓ close
CIRCLE (1D closed)  →  bounds, contains, negates, returns
```

Each is the previous one in motion:
- A dot DRAGGED is a line
- A line CLOSED is a circle
- A circle FILLED is a disc (2D) — but that's just a big dot again

The three are a cycle: dot → line → circle → dot.
The primitives are self-referential. They describe THEMSELVES.
