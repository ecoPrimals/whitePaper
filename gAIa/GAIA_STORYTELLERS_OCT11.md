## THE SCATTER

Light hits a surface. Most passes through or absorbs.
Some scatters BACK. Backscatter. The light that returns
carries information about the surface it hit. Radar works
this way. Ultrasound works this way. Sonar works this way.

**Stories are backscatter.**

A storyteller sends a signal into the world. Most of it
absorbs (people hear, forget, move on). Some scatters
back — changed. The story that returns to the teller is
NOT the story they sent. It carries the shape of every
surface it hit. Every listener. Every retelling. Every
culture it passed through.

The story that comes back is a FRESH EPITOPE. Not the
original epitope. A new one. Born from the collision
between the original signal and the surfaces it struck.

## LE GUIN WAS RIGHT: THE BAG, NOT THE ARROW

Le Guin's Carrier Bag Theory of Fiction (1986):

> "Before the tool that forces energy outward, we made
> the tool that brings energy home. Before the spear,
> the basket. Before the arrow, the bag."

The hero narrative is the ARROW. Linear. One protagonist.
Conflict → resolution. Kill the monster. Win the war.
Return triumphant. That's the spear.

The carrier bag narrative is the CONTAINER. Nonlinear.
Many things held together. Not one hero but a community
of objects, experiences, relationships. That's the bag.

**The epitope sorter is a carrier bag.**

It doesn't kill anything. It doesn't have a protagonist.
It doesn't arc toward resolution. It HOLDS things. It
CARRIES them. It puts 160 epitopes in one bag and carries
them from domain to domain. The bag gets heavier. The
bag gets richer. The bag never empties.

The arrow hits one target. The bag carries everything.

`gift_outlasts_empire` (#126) — the bag outlasts the arrow.
Every arrow eventually lands. Every bag keeps carrying.
