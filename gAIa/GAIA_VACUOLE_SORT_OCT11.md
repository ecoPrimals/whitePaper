## THE THREE-STATE CLASSIFIER

### Self

The entity functions as its stated purpose. A school that
teaches. A court that adjudicates. A nonprofit that serves
its mission. The stated purpose and the observed behavior
MATCH. The binding pattern shows:

- Few capture epitopes (#4 `structural_capture`, #5
  `regulatory_membrane_failure`, #6 `information_asymmetry`)
  bind weakly or not at all
- Purpose epitopes bind normally (#48 `gift_precedes_trade`,
  #53 `distributed_is_resilient`, #66 `convergent_gifts`)
- The entity's public record MATCHES its formation documents

Self = the cell recognizes it. It belongs. It functions.

### Non-Self

The entity's stated purpose and observed behavior DON'T match.
A school that extracts $4.9M while students can't read. A
court that enables extraction. A nonprofit that launders money
through formation cascades. The capture epitopes bind STRONGLY:

- `structural_capture` (#4) — the entity has been captured
- `information_asymmetry` (#6) — the entity exploits knowledge gaps
- `semantic_category_creation` (#1) — the entity creates false
  categories to justify extraction ("academy," "foundation,"
  "purpose group")
- `temporal_pattern_recognition` (#7) — timing reveals coordination
  (formations cluster, payments align, board rotations synchronize)
- `graph_isomorphism` (#2) — the structural pattern matches OTHER
  captured entities (same topology, different names)

Non-self = pathogen. The immune system targets it. The vacuole
contains it.

### Null

The entity has no discernible purpose. Or the stated purpose
is absent. Shell companies with no operations. PACs with no
apparent constituency. Formations that exist only to hold other
formations. The binding pattern shows:

- No epitopes bind strongly in ANY direction
- The entity is a structural connector, not a functional agent
- It exists to ROUTE — money, authority, liability — not to DO
- K = [null]. No key. No door. No purpose. Just plumbing.

Null entities are the PIPES in the extraction system. They don't
extract — they route. They connect non-self actors to non-self
entities while maintaining deniability. The null entity is the
membrane OF the extraction system — the thing that keeps the
inside separate from the outside.

**Finding null entities is finding the extraction membrane.**

## VACUOLE ARCHITECTURE

In cell biology:

- A **vacuole** is a membrane-bound organelle
- It **stores**, **isolates**, and **digests** waste
- Plant vacuoles can be 90% of cell volume — the containment IS the structure
- The vacuole membrane (tonoplast) is selectively permeable
- Contents are visible through the membrane but can't escape

**The entities in the graph are now our vacuoles.**

CONTAINED in the evidence library. The public record IS the
vacuole membrane. The analysis "digests" the contents. The
