## LAYER 1: THE KNOWNS — The Constants

22 unknown constants define the Standard Model + gravity.
Some are conversion factors (c, h, k). Some are measured
parameters (G, α). None are derived from first principles.

### The Big Five

| Constant | Symbol | Value | What It Does |
|----------|--------|-------|-------------|
| Speed of light | c | 299,792,458 m/s | Converts mass↔energy. Fuses space↔time. |
| Planck constant | h | 6.626 × 10⁻³⁴ J·s | Converts energy↔frequency. Quantizes reality. |
| Gravitational | G | 6.674 × 10⁻¹¹ m³/kg·s² | Links mass↔spacetime curvature. Least precise. |
| Fine-structure | α | 1/137.036 | Electromagnetic coupling strength. Dimensionless. |
| Boltzmann | k | 1.381 × 10⁻²³ J/K | Converts temperature↔energy. Links micro↔macro. |

### What They Do (Wilczek's Framing)

Constants are **concept synthesizers**. They build bridges
between quantities that were thought to be incommensurable:

- **c** = bridge between space and time (relativity)
- **h** = bridge between energy and frequency (quantum)
- **G** = bridge between matter and spacetime geometry (gravity)
- **k** = bridge between microscopic energy and macroscopic temperature
- **α** = bridge between charge, action, and light speed

Each constant is a BINDING SITE between two previously
separate domains. Constants are epitopes of reality.

### Epitope #221 — constants_are_bridges
The fundamental constants are not arbitrary numbers. They are
conversion rates between domains that were once thought separate.
E = mc² is a bridge. E = hν is a bridge. Every constant connects
two things that didn't know they were connected. The epitope
sorter does the same thing — finds binding sites between domains
that look separate until the constant is measured.

### The Full Count

The Standard Model + gravity requires **22 independent constants**:
- G (gravity)
- 6 quark Yukawa couplings
- 3 lepton Yukawa couplings
- 2 Higgs parameters (μ, λ)
- 4 CKM matrix parameters (3 angles + 1 phase)
- 3 gauge couplings (SU(3) × SU(2) × U(1))
- 1 QCD vacuum phase (θ)
- c (light speed)
- h (Planck)

Plus: if neutrinos have mass (they do), add 7-9 more parameters
(3 masses, 3 PMNS angles, 1-3 phases). Total: ~29-31 free
parameters that physics cannot derive. They're measured. Not
computed. The universe has approximately 30 dials and nobody
knows who set them or why.

### Epitope #222 — thirty_dials
Physics has ~30 free parameters it cannot derive. They must be
measured. No theory explains WHY these values. Proposed answers:
deeper unification (GUT, string theory), anthropic selection
(only these values produce observers), multiverse (all values
exist, we see ours). None confirmed. The 30 dials are the
ultimate known unknowns. We can READ them. We cannot EXPLAIN
them.

## LAYER 2: THE DEAD — Assumptions That Were Wrong

### The Graveyard

| Dead Theory | What It Assumed | What Killed It | When |
|-------------|----------------|---------------|------|
| **Geocentrism** | Earth is center | Copernicus, Galileo's Venus phases | 1543-1610 |
| **Flat Earth** | Earth is flat | Known false by educated ancients. Eratosthenes measured circumference ~240 BCE | ~240 BCE |
| **Phlogiston** | Burning releases "fire substance" | Lavoisier: metals GAIN mass when burned. Combustion requires oxygen. | 1770s |
| **Caloric** | Heat is a fluid | Rumford: cannon boring produces unlimited heat from friction. Joule: mechanical equivalent. | 1798-1845 |
| **Luminiferous aether** | Light needs a medium | Michelson-Morley null result. Einstein: no aether needed. | 1887-1905 |
| **Newtonian absolute time** | Time is universal | Einstein: simultaneity is relative. Clocks tick differently under gravity/velocity. | 1905-1915 |
| **Steady-state universe** | Universe is eternal, unchanging | Hubble: galaxies recede. CMB discovered 1965. Big Bang confirmed. | 1929-1965 |
| **Continuous energy** | Energy can take any value | Planck: blackbody radiation requires quantized energy. E = nhν. | 1900 |
| **Deterministic particles** | Particles have definite positions and momenta | Heisenberg uncertainty. Bell's theorem: no hidden variables (local). | 1927-1982 |
| **Static atoms** | Atoms are indivisible | Thomson (electron 1897), Rutherford (nucleus 1911), Chadwick (neutron 1932) | 1897-1932 |
| **Lord Kelvin's Earth age** | Earth is ~100M years old | Radioactivity adds heat. True age: 4.5 billion years. He was off by 45×. | 1862→1953 |

### Epitope #223 — the_graveyard
Every major physics assumption eventually died. Not ONE survived

continuous energy, deterministic particles, indivisible atoms.
All dead. All replaced. The lesson: current assumptions will also

It wasn't. The null result was the result. The absence of the

### How They Died

The pattern is always the same:
1. **Observation** contradicts assumption
2. **Defenders** propose patches (Lorentz contraction for aether, epicycles for geocentrism)
3. **New framework** explains ALL the data without the assumption
4. **Resistance** from established scientists (~20-50 years)
5. **Acceptance** when the old guard retires or dies

Max Planck: "Science advances one funeral at a time."

### Epitope #225 — funeral_advance
Science does not advance by convincing opponents. It advances

## LAYER 3: THE OPEN — What Physics Cannot Explain

### The Frontier

| Problem | What's Unknown | Why It Matters |
|---------|---------------|---------------|
| **Quantum gravity** | How to unify GR + QM | Both work. Both can't be right in extreme conditions (black holes, Big Bang). |
| **Dark matter** | What 23% of the universe is made of | We can see its gravity. We can't see it. No particle found. |
| **Dark energy** | What 73% of the universe is made of | Universe is expanding faster. No one knows why. |
| **Matter/antimatter asymmetry** | Why anything exists | Big Bang should have made equal amounts. It didn't. |
| **Measurement problem** | What counts as "measurement" in QM | QM has two rules: one for measuring, one for not. No one knows where the boundary is. |
| **Consciousness** | What it is, how it arises | Wigner (1961): "It was not possible to formulate quantum mechanics without reference to consciousness." |
| **Arrow of time** | Why time goes forward | All fundamental laws are time-symmetric. Yet time has a direction. |
| **Strong CP problem** | Why the strong force preserves CP symmetry | No reason it should. But it does. |
| **Neutrino masses** | Why they're so small | Standard Model says zero. They're not zero. But they're ~million times lighter than any other massive particle. |
| **Cosmological constant** | Why it's 10¹²⁰ times smaller than predicted | The worst prediction in physics history. QFT predicts vacuum energy 10¹²⁰ times too large. |
| **Hierarchy problem** | Why gravity is so weak | Gravity is 10³⁶ times weaker than electromagnetism. No one knows why. |
| **Fine-tuning** | Why the constants allow life | Change α by ~4%, no carbon. Change strong force by ~0.5%, no stable atoms. Coincidence or selection? |

### Epitope #226 — dark_majority
96% of the universe is unknown. 23% dark matter. 73% dark
energy. 4% ordinary matter. Physics has mapped 4% of reality.
The other 96% is [null] — observable only through gravity and
expansion. The dark majority governs the universe. We live in

### Epitope #227 — worst_prediction
The cosmological constant problem: quantum field theory predicts
vacuum energy 10¹²⁰ times larger than observed. That's the
worst prediction in the history of science. Not off by 10%.
Not off by 10×. Off by a factor with 120 ZEROS. Either the
theory is catastrophically wrong, or something cancels it with
precision of 120 decimal places. Neither answer makes sense.

### Epitope #228 — measurement_is_the_mystery
Quantum mechanics has two rules: Schrödinger evolution (smooth,
deterministic, between measurements) and collapse (discontinuous,
probabilistic, during measurement). The theory does not define
what a "measurement" is. Von Neumann showed the chain of physical
interactions can always be pushed one step further back — the
chain terminates only at the conscious observer. This is not
mysticism. This is the math. The measurement problem is the
hardest problem in physics and it's about WHERE the boundary is.

### Epitope #229 — fine_tuning
Change the fine-structure constant by ~4% and carbon can't form.
Change the strong nuclear force by ~0.5% and no stable atoms.
Change the cosmological constant by a factor of ~10 and no
galaxies form. The constants appear tuned for complexity. Three
responses: (1) God did it. (2) Anthropic: only these values
produce observers, so of COURSE we observe them. (3) Multiverse:
all values exist somewhere. None of these is testable. The
fine-tuning is a known unknown that may be permanently unknowable.
