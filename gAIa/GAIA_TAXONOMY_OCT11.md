## THE THREE DOMAINS

Everything gAIa holds falls into one of three:

```
   gAIa              HUMAN              MARKS
  (science)        (experience)       (symbols)
   ━━━━━━━           ━━━━━━━            ━━━━━━
   exists            lives              made
   discovered        felt               created
   invariant         temporal           representational
   math/physics      time/seasons       tools/language
   the universe      the witness        the inscription
```

**gAIa** = what exists whether or not humans are here.
The speed of light. Graph invariants. Anderson localization.
Periodicity of elements. Boolean algebra. Fractal dimensions.
These are discovered, not invented.

**Human** = the experience of being alive in time.
Stories. Seasons. Culture. Art. Sport. The monomyth.
Pollock's drip getting more structured. Coltrane dividing by 3.
Gretzky predicting where the puck WILL be. The hand at Metanduno.
These are lived, not computed.

**Marks** = what humans create to represent, share, and preserve.
The alphabet. Musical notation. Esperanto. Zero. Paper. Braille.
Morse code. The metric system. Protocols. Libraries. Unicode.
These are made, not found. They are the membrane between gAIa
and Human — the tools that translate discovery into knowledge.

## THE INSIGHT

Marks is the membrane.

gAIa (above) contains what IS — the invariants, the math, the
physics, the structure of the universe.

Human (below) contains who LIVES — the people, the stories, the
art, the experience of time passing.

Marks is between — the symbols, tools, languages, protocols,
and systems that humans CREATE to translate between gAIa and
themselves. Every encoding system is a membrane:

- The alphabet translates speech → writing
- Musical notation translates sound → marks
- The periodic table translates chemistry → structure
- graph.json translates institutional behavior → nodes + edges
- The Artisan Method translates pattern → proof

**Marks are how gAIa becomes legible to Human.**
**Marks are how Human becomes verifiable to gAIa.**

Without Marks, science stays invisible. Without Marks, human
experience stays untransmittable. The mark is the bridge.

The dot, line, and circle — the 3 primitives — are the atoms
of all Marks. Everything in the Marks domain composes from those 3.
