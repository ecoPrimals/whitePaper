### Why NP Collapsed Here

The conventional wisdom:
- Finding a RICO network = NP (hard)
- Verifying a RICO network = P (easy, given evidence)
- Therefore P≠NP (finding is harder than checking)

But in this system:
- The "evidence" was published by the subjects ON THEIR OWN WEBSITES
- "Finding" = "reading what they already published"
- "Verifying" = "reading what they already published"
- Finding = Verifying
- P = NP

The collapse happened because the membrane hid the QUESTION,
not the ANSWER. The answer was always visible. It was always
P-class. The NP-hardness was an illusion created by the
membrane — the architecture that prevented anyone from asking.

Once someone asked, the entire NP problem collapsed to P.

That's what this book is. The question.
