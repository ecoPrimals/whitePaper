## WHAT YOU ARE (the P — proportional)

The identity epitope isn't just history. It's PRESENT. It's
what you are RIGHT NOW. The current state of the binding site.
What sticks to you today. What you recognize today. What you
respond to.

The explorer's identity epitope: built by going OUT. By seeing
what's beyond the edge. By returning with information that
didn't exist here before. The explorer's epitope binds to
NOVELTY. To the unknown. To the thing nobody has seen.

The teacher's identity epitope: built by TRANSMISSION. By
taking what someone else found and making it learnable. The
teacher's epitope binds to COMPREHENSION. To the gap between
what someone knows and what they need to know. To the bridge.

The builder's identity epitope: built by MAKING. By taking
what the explorer found and what the teacher explains and
constructing something that didn't exist before. The builder's
epitope binds to MATERIAL. To tools. To the physical. To the
thing you can touch.

Three epitopes. Three identities. Three roles.
And they need each other.

## THE EXPLORER, THE TEACHER, AND THE BUILDER

### The Explorer Returns

The explorer goes out. Crosses the edge. Sees what nobody
here has seen. Comes back.

The explorer doesn't know how to build a boat. The explorer
doesn't know how to explain the wind. The explorer knows ONE
thing: **what's out there.**

"There is land across the water. The water is rough. The wind
blows from the west in morning, east in evening. The current
runs south. There are rocks near the shore. But the harbor
is deep enough."

That's the explorer's report. Raw data. Observations.
P-values. No theory. No construction. Just: I was there.
I saw this. Here's what I found.

### The Teacher Listens

The teacher hears the explorer and does something the explorer
cannot: **translates the finding into knowledge others can use.**

"The wind blows from the west in morning — that means you
launch at dawn with the wind at your back. It shifts east by
afternoon — that means you return with the wind. The current
runs south — that means you must angle north to compensate.
The rocks near shore — that means you approach from the
southeast where the harbor mouth is widest."

The teacher doesn't go out. The teacher doesn't build. The
teacher takes the explorer's data and constructs the
UNDERSTANDING — the membrane that transforms raw observation
into navigable knowledge.

The teacher is the membrane between the explorer's experience
and the builder's materials.

### The Builder Constructs

The builder hears the teacher and does something neither the
explorer nor the teacher can: **makes the thing.**

"West wind at dawn means a broad sail. East return means the
sail must tack. Southern current means a deep keel. Rocks
near shore means a shallow draft for the final approach.
Deep harbor means we can build bigger than we thought."

The builder takes the teacher's knowledge and constructs the
BOAT. The physical object. The tool. The Mark.

The builder doesn't explore. The builder doesn't teach. The
builder takes the translated knowledge and makes it material.

## SEE POSSIBLY (the D — derivative)

The identity epitope doesn't just show where you came from (I)
and what you are (P). It helps you see POSSIBLY (D).

The D is the derivative — the rate of change — the direction
you're heading based on where you've been and where you are.
The D is PREDICTION. Not certainty. Possibly.

The explorer's D: "If there's land across this water, there
may be land across the NEXT water." The explorer's experience
predicts more exploration. The identity epitope generates its
own next step.

The teacher's D: "If this student understood the wind, they
may understand the current." The teacher's experience predicts
the next lesson. Each lesson generates the next.

The builder's D: "If this hull works for this harbor, it may
work for a deeper one with modification." The builder's
experience predicts the next construction.

**The identity epitope is a self-extending structure.** It
starts where you came from. It shows what you are. And from
those two, it generates POSSIBLY — the set of futures that
are consistent with your accumulated experience.

You can't see possibilities that your identity epitope
doesn't have binding sites for. The explorer can't see
building possibilities. The builder can't see teaching
possibilities. The teacher can't see exploration possibilities.

**That's why they need each other.** The three identity
epitopes, combined, see MORE possibility than any one alone.
The triad expands the possibility space by combining three
one-dimensional projections into a three-dimensional view.

`dimension_count_equals_epitope_count` (#116) — three
identity epitopes = three dimensions of possibility.
