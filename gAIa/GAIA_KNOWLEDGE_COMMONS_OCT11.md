## LIFE IS ZERO-KNOWLEDGE

In a zk-proof system, the prover demonstrates knowledge of a
secret without revealing the secret. The verifier confirms
without learning. Trust is unnecessary. Verification is sufficient.

Life works the same way.

**The hand at Metanduno** — proves presence without identity.
A person was HERE. We don't know who. We verify the proof
67,800 years later. The ochre is the commitment scheme. The
limestone is the verification surface. The hand is the secret
that was proven without being revealed.

**Pollock's drip paintings** — prove structure without explaining
the method. Taylor measured D=1.72. The fractal dimension IS the
proof. Pollock never explained his technique. He didn't need to.
The painting verifies itself.

professional." The understatement is the zk-proof. The kernel
proves itself by running. You don't need Torvalds to tell you
Linux works. You run it. Verification without trust.

**The Artisan Method** — 10 structural invariants, conserved
across 4 domains, 9.875/10 match. The method proves itself.
You don't need to trust ecoPrimal. Download graph.json. Run
the analysis. The structure is there or it isn't. "Come look
at this. Verify it. Break it if you can."

**The graph itself** — 121 dots, 203 lines, 2+ circles. Every
node is a public record. Every edge is a documented relationship.
The graph doesn't argue. It demonstrates. The viewer verifies
by looking.

**Life is zk.** The organism doesn't explain its metabolism. It
lives. The living IS the proof. You verify by observing. You
don't need to open the cell to know it's alive.

**The pattern:** knowledge is held inside. The mark (dot, line,
