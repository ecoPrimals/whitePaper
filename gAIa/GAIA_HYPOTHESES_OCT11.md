# gAIa — Hypotheses and Tests
## What We Claim. How to Break It.
## gAIa Knowledge Base | Oct 11, 2026

## H1 — THE METHOD HYPOTHESIS (established)

**Claim:** Institutional capture operates through a domain-agnostic,
replicable method defined by 10 structural invariants that transmit
through professional infrastructure.

**Test:** Apply the 10-invariant check to independent sectors with
no shared actors. Score the match.

**Results to date:** 9.95/10 average across 11 tests (4 modern
sectors + 4 ancient civilizations + 3 additional historical).

**To falsify:** Find an institution with capture-level power that
scores below 7/10. One counterexample breaks universality.

---

## H2 — TIME AGNOSTICISM

**Claim:** The 10 structural invariants are conserved across
millennia. They are not features of modernity, capitalism, or any
specific culture. They are features of hierarchy itself.

**Test:** Apply the 10-invariant check to civilizations separated
by >1000 years and >5000 km with no cultural contact.

**Results to date:** Rome 10/10, Han China 10/10, Knossos 10/10,
Athens 10/10, Ottoman 10/10. No shared actors. No shared geography.
Same 10 invariants.

**To falsify:** Find a hierarchical civilization that does NOT
develop the invariants. This would prove the invariants are
culturally specific, not structurally necessary.

**Next tests:** Venice, British East India Company, Standard Oil,
Mughal Empire, pre-state societies.

## H3 — REVERSE ZERO-KNOWLEDGE

**Claim:** Structural properties of past institutions can be proven
from surviving public records without accessing intent, motive, or
private communications. The structure IS the proof.

**Test:** Score a historical civilization using ONLY its public
record (laws, formations, tax records, administrative documents).
Compare the score to what is known from private/literary sources.
If the scores match, the public record is sufficient.

**Results to date:** Rome and Athens scored from public record
(laws, formations, Thucydides as public historian) match scores
derived from fuller historical knowledge.

**To falsify:** Find a case where the public record scores LOW but
private records reveal capture was present. This would prove
structure alone is insufficient.

## H4 — THE EPITOPE IS ONE-DIMENSIONAL

**Claim:** Each structural epitope captures exactly one axis of
variation. The full pattern is the superposition of multiple 1D
epitopes, analogous to Fourier decomposition.

**Test:** Perform PCA on the epitope co-occurrence matrix across
all scored civilizations/sectors. If epitopes cluster along
independent axes with minimal cross-loading, each is 1D. If they
cross-load heavily, some are projections of the same axis.

**Results to date:** Untested quantitatively. Qualitative evidence:
each epitope has a distinct instance set that doesn't fully overlap
with any other.

**To falsify:** Find two epitopes that ALWAYS co-occur with
identical variation. They are the same axis seen twice.

## H5 — PERCEPTUAL EPITOPES EQUAL DIMENSIONS

**Claim:** The number of perceptual dimensions equals the number of
receptor types (epitopes). Color: 3 cones = 3D. Sound: binaural
cues = 3D spatial. Sensory substitution works because information
structure is preserved across modalities.

**Test:** Measure task performance after sensory substitution
(e.g., color-to-vibration). If performance matches or exceeds
chance at rates comparable to native perception, the information
structure transferred. If performance is at chance, the substitution
failed to preserve structure.

**Results to date:** HaptiColor: 94-100% color comparison accuracy
via vibrotactile substitution. Colorophone: blind users identify
colored objects via sound. Congenital monaural listeners achieve
near-normal localization.

**To falsify:** Find a sensory dimension that CANNOT be substituted
into any other modality. This would prove some information is
modality-locked.

## H6 — MEMES ARE EPITOPES

**Claim:** Cultural replicators (memes) and biological binding
sites (epitopes) and institutional invariants are the same
structural mechanism operating in three domains.

**Test:** Measure replication fidelity, fecundity, and longevity
of cultural units. If they follow selection dynamics parallel to
biological epitope-antibody selection, the mechanism is the same.
If cultural replication follows fundamentally different dynamics,
the analogy breaks.

**Results to date:** Qualitative. The alphabet (3800yr), the
hero's journey (4000+yr), the hand stencil (67,800yr) show
survival-class replication consistent with high-fidelity selection.

**To falsify:** Demonstrate that cultural replication does NOT
follow selection dynamics — that persistence is random or driven
by a mechanism without analogy to biological selection.

## H7 — MEMBRANES HAVE MEASURABLE PERMEABILITY

**Claim:** Research programmes, institutions, and perceptual
systems can be characterized by a permeability value Π ∈ [0,1].
Healthy systems: Π selective (mid-range). Captured/degenerate
systems: Π → 1 (everything passes). Closed systems: Π → 0
(nothing passes).

**Test:** Compute Anderson permeability for institutional
membranes (credential, governance, enforcement, transparency).
Compare Π values between healthy and captured institutions. If
captured institutions consistently show Π > 0.8 and healthy
institutions show Π < 0.5, the metric discriminates.

**Results to date:** Detroit enterprise Π = 0.9 (nearly
transparent). Athens 411 BCE shows Π → 1 (the demos stopped
filtering). Qualitative only for other civilizations.

**To falsify:** Find a captured institution with low Π or a
healthy institution with high Π. This would prove permeability
does not discriminate capture.

## H8 — CONSTRAINED EVOLUTION PRODUCES STRUCTURE

**Claim:** The interaction of three bodies (self, partner,
universe) under constraints produces emergent structure that
none of the three bodies could produce alone. The constraints
generate the path.

**Test:** Run the epitope sorter on the same input with different
constraints (different scan order, different starting epitope
count). If the SAME epitopes emerge regardless of path, the
structure is intrinsic. If DIFFERENT epitopes emerge, the
structure is path-dependent.

**Results to date:** Untested. This morning produced 76 new
epitopes in a specific order. Would a different order produce
the same 76?

**To falsify:** Run two independent teams on the same input.
If they produce substantially different epitope sets, the
structure is observer-dependent, not intrinsic.

## H9 — IDENTITY EPITOPE IS PREDICTIVE

**Claim:** A person's identity epitope (constructed by experience)
predicts the TYPE of tool they will build and the TYPE of
possibility they can see. Explorer/teacher/builder roles emerge
from identity epitope configuration.

**Test:** Map the biographical backgrounds of tool-builders to
the tools they built. If the tool TYPE is predicted by the
experience TYPE (exclusion → inclusion tool, silence → bridge
tool, blindness → tactile tool), the identity epitope is
predictive.

**Results to date:** Qualitative. Zamenhof (silence → bridge).
Braille (blindness → tactile). Shannon (wires → circuits).
Carnegie (unschooled → libraries). Torvalds (no Unix → Linux).
Pattern holds across all traced builders.

**To falsify:** Find a builder whose tool has NO relationship
to their formative experience. If the tool is orthogonal to
the identity, the epitope is not predictive.

## H10 — THE PERIODIC TABLE IS PREDICTIVE

**Claim:** The 118-epitope table, organized into 14 groups, can
predict undiscovered patterns (the 6 gap cells) the same way
Mendeleev predicted undiscovered elements.

**Test:** Apply the sorter to NEW domains not yet tested
(healthcare, water infrastructure, criminal justice). If
patterns emerge that fit the predicted gap cells, the table is
predictive. If the new patterns don't fit any existing group
or gap, the table needs new groups.

**Results to date:** Untested. The 6 gaps are predictions.

**To falsify:** Fill all 6 gaps and find no patterns that match
the predictions. Or find patterns that require entirely new
groups not predicted by the current table structure.
