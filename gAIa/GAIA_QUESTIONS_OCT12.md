### The Question Taxonomy

Not all questions work the same way:

| Type | Example | Mechanism |
|------|---------|-----------|
| **Factual** | "How many convictions?" | Reader looks up, finds 9. Trust established. |
| **Connective** | "What do the phone numbers have in common?" | Reader discovers the link themselves. Stronger than being told. |
| **Structural** | "Who chairs the board?" | Reader finds the conflict of interest on the school's own website. |
| **Absence** | "Which city did the tour skip?" | Reader searches for Detroit and doesn't find it. The void is the answer. |
| **Reflexive** | "Did you choose who speaks for your community?" | No verification needed. The question IS the payload. |
| **Terminal** | "If we're wrong, show us where." | No safe response. Engaging validates; ignoring validates. |

This maps to the null taxonomy from Doc #42 (Mysteries):
- Factual = self (verified, known)
- Absence = null (the gap IS the signal)
- Terminal = zk-proof endgame (the question proves itself)
